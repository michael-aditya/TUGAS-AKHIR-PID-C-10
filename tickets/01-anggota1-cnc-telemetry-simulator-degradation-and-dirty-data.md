# 01: [Anggota 1] CNC Telemetry Simulator, 7-Day Gradual Degradation Generator & Out-of-Band Dirty Data Injector

**What to build:** Deliver the core CNC production line telemetry generator and fault injection capability (Seam 1: `generate_telemetry_batch`) for 10 **Machine** assets (`CNC-01` through `CNC-10`) and 30 **Device** endpoints (10 `vibration`, 10 `spindle`, and 10 `production_counter` devices). The generator produces realistic physical telemetry readings across a 7-day timeline—modeling **Gradual Degradation** on at least three **Machine** assets (`CNC-03` breaching the `7.1 mm/s` **Critical Vibration Threshold** and entering **Unplanned Downtime** `DOWN` on Day 6, `CNC-07` actively degrading at `+0.045 mm/s/hour` into the `< 48h` **Remaining Useful Life (RUL)** window, and `CNC-09` exhibiting early wear at `+0.030 mm/s/hour`, while the remaining 7 **Machine** assets operate stably)—and intentionally injects six configurable **Dirty Data** categories (`missing_value`, `duplicate`, **Out-of-Range Spike**, **Stuck Sensor**, `inconsistent_format`, and `late_event`) while recording exact ground-truth fault counts in an out-of-band **Injection Audit Log** without adding any dirty-data marker to the telemetry payload itself. Includes base messaging and raw storage container definitions (`mosquitto` and `mongodb`) and automated tests verifying Seam 1.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Emits valid **Telemetry Reading** records containing `device_id`, `device_type`, `machine_id`, `timestamp`, and metric `data` across all 10 **Machine** assets and 30 **Device** endpoints (`CNC-01`..`CNC-10` × `VIB`, `SPD`, `PRD`).
- [ ] Reproduces the 7-day **Gradual Degradation** trajectories: `CNC-03` reaches `>= 7.1 mm/s` and transitions its **Operational Status** to `DOWN` on Day 6; `CNC-07` rises steadily over the final 72 hours to `~5.8 mm/s` (`+0.045 mm/s/h`); `CNC-09` rises gradually to `~4.6 mm/s` (`+0.030 mm/s/h`); the other 7 **Machine** assets stay within normal operating ranges (`0.5–3.5 mm/s`, `30–70 °C`, `0–12000 rpm`).
- [ ] Injects all 6 configurable **Dirty Data** categories (`missing_value`, `duplicate`, **Out-of-Range Spike** `> 25.0 mm/s` or `< 0`, **Stuck Sensor** runs of `>= 4` consecutive identical float values, `inconsistent_format` status/timestamp strings, and `late_event` delayed timestamps).
- [ ] Logs every intentionally injected fault into the out-of-band **Injection Audit Log** while keeping the **Telemetry Reading** JSON payload free of any dirty-data indicator flags (per ADR-0001).
- [ ] Automated test suite exercises the Seam 1 generator interface to verify 30-device coverage, degradation trajectories, payload purity, and exact reconciliation between injected faults and the **Injection Audit Log**.
